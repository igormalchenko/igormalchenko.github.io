# Concert of Europe — local logo exploration

Three concept proofs generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 8 November 2026.
The briefs are preserved verbatim in `prompts.json`.
Open `index.html` from disk to compare the proofs.

1. **The Congress Table** — five chairs gathered around a European map. Recommended: the most direct visual expression of the congress system. The current proof is `01-congress-table-v2.png`: five distinct interior chair-back designs in the large emblem, five matching simplified chairs in the small marks. The original is preserved for comparison. The exact built-in ImageGen edit prompt is in `01-congress-table-v2-prompt.txt`.
2. **The Five Seals** — a treaty with five diplomatic seals. A formal document-led alternative.
3. **The Pentarchy** — five shields around Europe. The heraldic devices are invented abstractions, not the actual national arms of the five powers.

Historical basis: the Concert's five-power system comprised Austria, Britain, Prussia, Russia and France. See the [Austrian Federal Chancellery's Congress of Vienna account](https://www.bundeskanzleramt.gv.at/en/federal-chancellery/visit-us/history/the-congress-of-vienna.html).
All three are original game identity proposals, not reproductions of a historical organization logo.

These PNGs are design proofs, not production logos or favicon exports. Their miniature examples illustrate a direction and do not validate readability at 16 pixels. The selected design needs a clean vector redraw, simplified small-size mark, transparent assets and actual-size verification before integration.

The approved v2 congress-table direction is now implemented in the game. Shipping artwork and its production prompt live in `assets/brand/`; these original proof boards remain here for reference.
