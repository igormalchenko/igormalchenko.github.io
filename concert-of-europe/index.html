How it works

The ambition. Every nation has one ambition for the armistice of 1918: the great powers of 1836 want the most gold a year, a divided people wants to unite and hold together, and the small want to survive. Each season its ruler forecasts what every option would add or protect, in gold over the years he plans ahead, and does everything that pays, best first. A province of the people it is uniting is worth twice its gold a year to it, and a nation that only has to survive counts every loss half again as dear.

value = extra gold a year × years planned − cost − expected losses

Factories pay for good but each one costs more and adds less. An army deters neighbours whose own forecast says "invade", but its men are workers missing from fields and factories. A war is worth chance of winning × what the province pays once it is integrated, minus army losses, blockaded trade and neighbours' distrust. Great powers may step in, especially against whoever is leading.

What throws the plans off. Rulers misjudge their odds, and each carries a trait with a bonus and a malus. Successions can become civil wars, rivals send ultimatums, coal booms make provinces into prizes, coups topple restless governments, and military revolutions swing battles. Subject peoples awaken and rise when their rulers' armies are busy; factory workers grow restless late in the century. A people that wins its uprising brings back its fallen nation or forms a new one, which claims the provinces of the union it works toward.

Map key

Click a province to inspect it; its nation fills the left column, with factories, fortresses and unrest drawn on its provinces. Click the sea or press Escape to go back to your own nation (or the leader). If you rule a nation, you choose its ruler at the start and at every succession, and permanent development cards on schedule; its ministers decide everything else. Keys: the number on a card to pick that card, R to reroll heirs, A for the Almanac, Space or P to play or pause, + and − to change speed. Click the world number to start a new world.
