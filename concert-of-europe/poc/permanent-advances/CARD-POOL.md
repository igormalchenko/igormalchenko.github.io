# Permanent advances: refreshed card pool

Thirty cards. Seven permanent choices per game, starting in 1836 and every twelve years through 1908. The governor, offers, geography filters, stacking and UI are unchanged.

Standalone recruitment and upkeep discounts have been replaced. Some cards combine effects to change several parts of an existing forecast. War Ministry is a deliberately strong POC option; the pool is ready for playtesting rather than claimed to be balanced.

## Military and defence

### General Staff

Armies fight 50% harder. Battle casualties are 35% lower.

Raises estimated victory odds and reduces the forecast cost of fighting with the army you already have.

### Rifled Artillery

Armies fight 60% harder.

Improves victory odds in the invasion forecast and strength in actual battles.

### Army Medical Corps

Armies fight 20% harder. Battle casualties are 60% lower.

Makes campaigns less costly while improving the army’s chances of winning.

### Campaign Administration

Armies fight 35% harder. Conquests integrate 150% faster.

Combines better combat odds with an earlier economic return from conquest.

### Border War College

Armies fight 80% harder against the named neighbour only. Battle casualties are 25% lower in all wars.

Improves the forecast against the named neighbour. Lower casualties also make other campaigns less costly.

### National Defence

Armies fight 80% harder in wars over your nation’s own land. Fortresses cost 60% less.

Changes the estimated balance in wars over national land and makes threatened borders cheaper to fortify.

### Frontier Engineers

Fortresses cost 75% less. Armies fight 20% harder.

Makes border fortification much cheaper and also improves military odds.

### Staff Colleges

Ministers plan twelve more years ahead. Armies fight 40% harder.

Counts more years of a conquest’s returns while improving victory odds. Long-term civilian investments also become more attractive.

### Expeditionary Service

Armies fight 50% harder. Other peoples’ provinces integrate 200% faster.

Improves combat odds and makes foreign conquests repay their costs sooner.

### War Ministry

Armies fight 60% harder and upkeep is 65% lower. Conquests integrate 200% faster. Ministers plan twenty more years ahead.

Makes campaigns cheaper to maintain, improves victory odds and counts more of a conquest’s future returns. Also changes civilian investment forecasts.

## Industry and commerce

### Machine Tools

Factories cost 45% less.

Directly lowers factory prices and brings more industrial investments within reach.

### Steam Shipping

Sea trade earns 80% more.

Makes coastal earnings more valuable. Ministers also count more lost trade when weighing a war.

### Industrial Credit

Factories cost 25% less. Ministers plan ten more years ahead.

Lowers the initial investment and counts more years of factory returns.

### Railway Procurement

Factories cost 30% less. Fortresses cost 50% less.

Makes both industrial construction and border defence more affordable.

### Merchant Banks

Sea trade earns 50% more. Taxes earn 30% more.

Changes the value of commerce and taxable provinces, while increasing funds for investment.

### Industrial Standards

All earnings rise by 20%. Factories cost 20% less.

Raises the forecast return on factories as well as reducing their price.

### Export Houses

Sea trade earns 45% more. Conquests integrate 80% faster.

Strengthens peaceful trade and brings the rewards of new territory forward. Ministers weigh both effects.

### Land Survey

Taxes earn 60% more.

Increases the forecast value of taxable provinces, both at home and across the border.

### Domestic Markets

Accepted peoples’ taxes rise by 80%. Factories cost 15% less.

Makes investment cheaper and increases the tax value of provinces whose people your nation accepts.

### Precision Engineering

Factories cost 25% less. Armies fight 30% harder.

Improves industrial investment and military forecasts together.

## Government

### Professional Civil Service

All earnings rise by 22%.

Raises the returns ministers expect from economic improvements and taxable territory.

### Parliamentary Procedure

Reforms cost 60% less.

Directly lowers the price ministers compare against the expected benefits of reform.

### Public Institutions

Reforms remove 120% more unrest.

Makes reform more valuable where unrest is hurting production or threatening an uprising.

### Regional Administration

Conquests integrate 200% faster.

Brings the economic rewards of a possible conquest much closer in the invasion forecast.

### Provincial Rights

Other peoples’ provinces integrate 300% faster. Reforms remove 35% more unrest.

Makes foreign conquests pay sooner and gives ministers a stronger response to unrest.

### Public Accounts

Taxes earn 30% more. Reforms cost 35% less.

Increases the value recovered by calming unrest while lowering the cost of reform.

### National Schools

Ministers plan ten more years ahead. Accepted peoples’ taxes rise by 30%.

Makes long-term investments and provinces with accepted peoples more valuable in the forecast.

### Technical Universities

Ministers plan six more years ahead. All earnings rise by 15%.

Raises and extends the forecast returns from investment and territorial gains.

### Civic Mediation

Reforms cost 35% less and remove 60% more unrest.

Changes both sides of the reform calculation: a smaller expense for a larger benefit.

### Consular Service

Conquests integrate 130% faster. Sea trade earns 30% more.

Balances earlier returns from new territory against the increased value of peaceful commerce.
