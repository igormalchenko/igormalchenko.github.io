The supply

Collection, storage and distribution of product in the national network. Bureau of Domestic Supply.

The Bureau of Domestic Supply operates a national network for the collection, storage and distribution of blood, referred to below as product. The network has been in continuous operation since 1951 and uses no infrastructure built for the purpose.

Found in a box of transfer files. No cover sheet. No signature.

Delivery is made quarterly to the recipients, in a quantity they set. The allocation has risen in each of the last eleven years. The most recent quarter was under-delivered by sixteen per cent.

“The recipients.” Never defined. Not once in the whole file.

Collection § 1

Hospital sampling

Samples drawn for diagnosis exceed the volume any test requires, typically by a factor of three. The surplus is not returned to the patient and is not destroyed. It is recorded as consumed in testing and removed from the site overnight.

Mum’s bloods in the spring. Four tubes for a cholesterol test.

Requests for the return of a sample are refused on grounds of contamination. This is the practice in every accredited laboratory and was already the practice before the network existed.

Menstrual recovery

Tampax and Kotex hold most of the market between them. Both formulate their absorbent to release under a wash the Bureau specifies, and both consign their consolidated waste to a single haulier.

The two firms are older than anything else in the network. Collection was first assigned in antiquity to two men, Tampaxos and Kotexos, and the houses they founded have carried on the same work without interruption since. Both still trade under shortened forms of their founders’ names.

I know this vase. School trip. I always thought it was wine.

Recovery runs at about six units in ten; the remainder stays bound in the absorbent. Raising the figure would require altering the products, which is not currently proposed. This is the larger of the two streams and the poorer in quality.

Storage § 2

Breweries and wineries

The reserve is held in fermentation tanks on licensed brewing and winemaking sites. A tank of this kind is opaque, chilled, sealed, guarded and plumbed to the street, and on such a site none of that requires explanation.

The sites brew and bottle. The output is genuine and comes to roughly a twelfth of rated capacity. Every large brewery and winery in the country is held by the Bureau through intermediaries.

Did the tour Saturday. The guide said “two hundred and ten thousand litres” and then moved us along quite fast.

Sites are selected for rail access and for the volume of tanker traffic a brewery can receive without remark.

Counted them from the car park afterwards. Forty-one.

Distribution § 3

The mains

Product moves through the gas network. Gas compresses and can be carried in a narrow pipe at high pressure; product cannot be compressed and needs a bore several times wider for the same throughput. Any main above roughly 300 millimetres carries product.

The difference is apparent in an open trench and no way of concealing it has been found. Works filings record every main at its fuel classification.

They dug up Halden Road in June. The pipe came up past the workman’s knees. “Gas,” he said.

Electric ranges

A district is converted to electric cooking before its mains are reassigned. The programme is published as modernisation and the appliances are fitted at Bureau expense.

After conversion the main under the street stays live and pressurised and serves no premises. Meters continue to register domestic consumption and billing continues unchanged. What a district cooks on therefore indicates what its mains carry.

Typing § 4

Blood groups

The groups were introduced in 1951 and have no basis in physiology. Any unit is compatible with any patient.

1951. Same year the network starts. I’m O negative. I have always been told I’m O negative.

The classification allows a refusal to be issued as a finding. Where a district’s reserve falls below the working minimum, cross-match requests from that district return no compatible unit. The determination is made at the registry rather than in the laboratory, and the hospital is answered in the language of typing.
